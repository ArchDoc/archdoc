The explorer's Plan tab shows only what the plan touches (its intents, changes, and sequences, with what they connect to), with the whole system a tap away. An intent can be about how two boxes connect: click an arrow, or drag from one box to another, and it's pinned between them (`to:` beside `on:`). The agent's questions can carry `options`, shown as buttons: one tap answers, settles, and sends it.
